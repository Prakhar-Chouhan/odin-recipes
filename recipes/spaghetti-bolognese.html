Spaghetti Bolognese

Description

This spaghetti Bolognese recipe is a classic Italian dish made with ground meat, tomatoes, and a blend of aromatic vegetables. It's a hearty and satisfying meal that's perfect for any occasion.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz spaghetti
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 can (14 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 1 celery stalk, chopped
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh basil (optional)

Steps

  1. Cook the spaghetti according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a large skillet, sauté onion, garlic, carrot, and celery until softened.
  3. Add ground beef and cook until browned. Drain excess fat.
  4. Stir in crushed tomatoes and simmer for 15-20 minutes. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Serve sauce over spaghetti and garnish with fresh basil if desired.
